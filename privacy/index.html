Privacy Policy

Effective 18 November 2026

WordGlance helps you learn English words from your Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

In short

  • No account, no sign-in.
  • No tracking and no analytics. Ads are optional short videos, only if you choose to watch one, and never with WordGlance Pro.
  • We (WordGlance) don't collect or receive your personal data.
  • Your progress and your own words stay on your device.

What stays on your device

The app saves the following on your iPhone so it can work. It isn't sent to us.

  • Your study progress: the current unit, words seen and known, daily counts and streak.
  • Your settings: daily goal, reminder time, auto-advance.
  • The words you add to My Words and their translations.
  • The date you started the free trial, kept in the device's Keychain so the trial can't be restarted.

The app and its widgets share this data through an App Group on your device. Deleting the app deletes it. The trial date may stay in the Keychain after the app is deleted.

Online translation

When you add a word to My Words with "Translate automatically" on (a Pro feature), WordGlance first looks the word up offline, in its built-in word lists and dictionary. Only if the word isn't found there does it send that English word to the MyMemory translation service (mymemory.translated.net, run by Translated srl) to get a Hebrew translation.

  • Only the word you typed is sent, over an encrypted connection.
  • No name, account, device identifier or other personal data is sent. As with any internet request, MyMemory can see your device's IP address.
  • MyMemory's own privacy terms apply to that request.
  • To keep everything offline, turn off "Translate automatically" and type the Hebrew yourself.

Apart from the optional videos below, this is the app's only network request. The widgets never connect to the internet.

Optional videos (ads)

Free users can choose to watch a short video to get 5 more words in My Words, or to save a missed day of their streak. The videos come from Google AdMob (Google LLC).

  • Nothing plays unless you tap "Watch a short video". There are no other ads, no banners, and no ads in the widgets.
  • With WordGlance Pro or the free trial there are no ads at all.
  • The ads are non-personalized: WordGlance doesn't ask to track you, doesn't use the advertising identifier (IDFA), and doesn't share your words or progress with Google.
  • To show a video and count it, Google's SDK receives information such as your IP address, device type and settings, and how you interact with the ad. Google uses it as described in its policy: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • In the EU and UK, the app asks for consent first, as the law requires. You can change your choice in Settings → About → Ad Privacy Choices.

Purchases

The WordGlance Pro subscription is sold and billed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The app checks your subscription with Apple's StoreKit on your device. Apple's privacy policy applies to your purchases.

Reminders

If you turn on the daily reminder, notifications are scheduled on your device. They aren't sent from a server.

Children

WordGlance doesn't collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Your choices

  • Settings → Reset Progress clears your study progress.
  • Delete words from My Words at any time.
  • Turn off "Translate automatically" to stay fully offline.
  • Delete the app to remove its data from your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app with a new effective date.
