The app

WordGlance shows English words with Hebrew translations in the app and in widgets, to help you learn vocabulary. You may use it for your own personal, non-commercial learning.

Free and Pro

  • Free: the course units, the widgets, the daily goal and up to 7 words in My Words, typed in yourself.
  • WordGlance Pro: unlimited words in My Words and automatic Hebrew translation when adding a word.

Optional videos

Free users can choose to watch a short video (an ad):

  • +5 words: each video lets you add 5 more words to My Words beyond the 7 free ones, up to 3 videos a day. The 5 words are used up as you add them. Deleting words doesn't give them back; the next words need another video. The 7 free words always stay free.
  • Save your streak: a video counts one missed day as done, so your streak continues.
  • Rewards count only after the video finishes, and they can't be exchanged for money or moved to another device. Video availability depends on the ad provider and isn't guaranteed.
  • With WordGlance Pro or the free trial there are no videos: words are unlimited and saving the streak is free.

Free trial

You can try Pro free for 7 days from inside the app. The trial needs no payment and no card, and it doesn't turn into a paid subscription. When it ends, the app goes back to Free. Every word you added stays, but adding more than 7 needs Pro. Each person can start the trial once.
